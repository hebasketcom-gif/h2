About Dr. Esha Kumari

Dr. Esha Kumari is an accomplished dental surgeon with advanced postgraduate specialization in Clinical Embryology. Driven by a passion for reproductive biology and cutting-edge Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART), she bridges clinical dentistry with micro-manipulation techniques, embryo culture, and preimplantation genetic diagnostics.

Her clinical ethos centers around patient-focused empathy, scientific precision, and continuous innovation in women's reproductive health and cellular research.

Scientific Focus

Research & Innovation Interests

01 / RESEARCH

Clinical Embryology & Oocyte Biology

Embryo Culture & Microfluidics

03 / RESEARCH

IVF Technology & Timelapse Systems

Applying AI-assisted morphokinetic tracking and time-lapse imaging to select highest-potential blastocysts.

04 / RESEARCH

Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT)

Genetic Screening & Biomarkers
