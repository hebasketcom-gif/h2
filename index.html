Dr. Esha Kumari is a highly accomplished healthcare professional whose career reflects a profound dedication to patient care, micro-surgical precision, and advanced reproductive medicine. With a strong clinical foundation established through her Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS), she has expanded her clinical horizon into the specialized domain of human embryology.

Her master's degree in MSc Clinical Embryology represents a deep academic commitment to understanding gamete biology, in-vitro fertilization (IVF) protocols, preimplantation genetic screening, and embryonic culture conditions. Her interdisciplinary expertise uniquely positions her at the intersection of precision surgical skill and modern reproductive science.

Driven by a passion for continuous learning and scientific rigor, Dr. Kumari aims to contribute significantly to the evolution of Assisted Reproductive Technologies (ART), ensuring that patient care is delivered with maximum accuracy, ethical responsibility, and compassionate excellence.

Research Interests

In-depth academic research focus areas in modern embryology, genetics, and reproductive technologies.

01

Clinical Embryology

Investigating gamete maturation, oocyte quality determinants, culture media formulations, and optimized incubation parameters to support early embryonic viability.

02

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)

Refining fertilization protocols, sperm selection methodologies (IMSI/PICSI), ICSI micromanipulation efficacy, and laboratory quality control measures.

03

PGT Genetics

Preimplantation Genetic Testing for aneuploidies (PGT-A) and monogenic disorders (PGT-M) using Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) to improve implantation rates.

Embryo Development

Morphokinetic profiling using time-lapse imaging systems (TLI) to track cleavage milestones, blastocyst formation, and non-invasive embryo grading.

05

Fertility Science

Studying reproductive endocrinology, endometrial receptivity windows, uterine microbiome influences, and fertility preservation modalities.

06

Modern ART Innovations

Exploring artificial intelligence applications in embryo selection, microfluidic sperm sorting, and ultra-rapid vitrification protocols.
